Measured twice, officially, at 124 centimetres — reported as among the longest women's legs in the world, and the longest ever recorded on a Slovenian. They are also the engine of an international modelling career.

The measurement

“I have a Thai modelling agency to thank — they measured me.”

She would never have known herself. A Thai agency took the measurement, twice, officially: 124 centimetres each time. Slovenian media have since reported it as the fourth-longest in the world.

“I'm not actually that tall — 184 centimetres — but I have a shorter torso and longer legs. The current record holder's legs measure 134 cm, yet she stands 208 cm tall. Relative to my size, mine are longer — so I'll have to have that checked again,” she laughs. Watch this space.

On the record. The 124 cm figure comes from two measurements taken by a Thai modelling agency, and the “fourth longest” ranking from Slovenian press coverage. Guinness World Records maintains a single official longest legs (female) title, awarded through its own adjudication process — a separate procedure this measurement has not been through. If it ever is, this page will be the first to say so.

/02 — THE MODELLING YEARS
Teens · Slovenia

First runways

A tall teenager stepping onto Slovenian catwalks, sharing fashion shows with well-known Slovenian and international modelling names — proof that brains and beauty walk hand in hand: she was an engineering student at the time.

Late teens · Milan

High-fashion Milan

Life in Milan as a fashion model and photo model for high fashion. Italian came along “as a hobby — so I could understand what people around me were saying.” As you do.

Las Vegas · USA

Miss Hawaiian Tropic

Represented Slovenia at the international Miss Hawaiian Tropic competition in Las Vegas — the first stamp in a pageant passport that would soon include Miss Universe, where she placed as second attendant.

007 · Quantum of Solace

The Bond girl

The only person chosen without an audition as an extra for James Bond: Quantum of Solace. A few seconds on screen — a lifetime of stories off it.

German TV

“Sag die Wahrheit”

Invited onto the popular German TV show as a special guest and introduced to the nation as “Ramona, the Bond girl.” A proud moment — and proof the Bond seconds travelled far.

2025 · Planet TV

Kolo sreče

Contestant on Slovenia's Wheel of Fortune, charming the studio with an infectious smile, confidence and — of course — the famous legs.

Brazil

The athlete detour

Her passion for sport took those record legs to athletics competitions in Brazil, where she competed as an athlete between studio sessions and club gigs.

Asia

The tall attraction

In Indonesia and Singapore, towering over average local heights, the 184 cm frame plus 124 cm legs made her not just a singer but a walking headline — and opened doors to stages across Asia.

/03 — THE BOND FILES

“I remember Daniel Craig, with those beautiful eyes, sitting on a chair right across from me. I was about to go over and ask him something when a security guard quickly stopped me: ‘Sorry, miss, he's in his role now, you mustn't disturb him.’”

Then there was director Marc Forster, trying to squeeze past her on set: “Because I have long legs, he couldn't get by. He joked: ‘Girl, I still have a film to shoot.’ We all laughed at how I'd accidentally blocked his path.”

Warmest memory: villain actor Anatole Taubman. “When I told him I was from Slovenia, he smiled — he used to have a Slovenian girlfriend. He said Slovenian women are very beautiful — of course, I know that — and then joked: ‘If you give me a kiss on the cheek, I'll tell you a Slovenian word.’”

The word was “hvala”.Thank you — appropriately
