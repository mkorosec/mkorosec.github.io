Note on the language inventory. HYPIA's own member page lists the levels exactly as the Story page shows them. Two labels differ: HYPIA records “Serbo-Croatian”, shown here as “Croatian / Serbian”, and “Sudanese”, shown here as Sundanese — the language of West Java, which is almost certainly what was meant, since Sudanese is a nationality rather than a language.
Note on the leg measurement. The 124 cm figure was measured twice by a Thai modelling agency. The “fourth longest in the world” ranking originates in Slovenian press coverage, not in a Guinness World Records adjudication — GWR maintains a single official longest legs (female) title awarded through its own process. This site states the measurement and attributes the ranking rather than claiming the title.

Biography
free to quote

/02 — SHORT & LONG

Short — 40 words

Ramona Irgolič is a German-Slovenian singer, model and hyperpolyglot. She sings in 50 languages, is fluent in ten, and has explored 80 countries and lived in ten of them. She is member No. 420 of HYPIA, the International Association of Hyperpolyglots.

Long — 120 words

Born in Mainz to Slovenian parents and raised near Maribor from the age of ten, Ramona Irgolič trained as an engineer — earning an MSc in technical environmental protection — before a move to southern Brazil in 2013 turned her into a professional singer. Discovered by Adriana Deffenti, she toured Asia with The Platters' James Sampson, became a familiar face in Jakarta, and performed her own songs on Singapore's Esplanade on two consecutive nights. In parallel she modelled in Milan, competed in Las Vegas, and appeared as an extra in Quantum of Solace. She learns languages by singing them first and understanding them later: fluent in ten, able to converse in twenty-five, and singing in fifty.
